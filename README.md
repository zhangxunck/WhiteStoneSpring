### 读之前要知道的

1. **每篇文章开头都挂了「数据底座」**：文中所有百分比、密度、极差、引语出处，均可溯源与复算。
2. **观点有清晰的边界**：凡属假说或经验归纳的，明确区分证据级别；不把个案特征包装成普遍规律。
3. **中英对照**：每篇都提供简中／繁中／英文三个版本，默认并排对照，顶部可切换。
4. **配图统一采用 HTML 原生排版**：
   - 彻底告别位图模糊与缩放失真，正文内嵌统一采用纯 **HTML / SVG 原生渲染**；
   - 支持高分屏、文字可选、任意缩放，点击即可独立全屏浏览；
   - 全部配图均脱敏去除了私有痕迹，统一署名 `@zhangxunnj`。

## English Introduction

> *"It's all made up, but you get to make it up."*  
> A continuous inquiry into language, prose, and thought.

**White Stone Spring (白石溪)** is an open-access repository and publication venue dedicated to long-form essays on Chinese copywriting standards, historical linguistic shifts, and cognitive mechanisms in the era of artificial intelligence. Every essay is strictly grounded in **reproducible quantitative data and primary sources**, rejecting vacuous rhetoric in favor of verifiable corpus metrics.

### The Writing Series: Four Pieces, One Chain

The currently running series has a single theme — **using writing to learn and develop yourself**. The four pieces are not four independent topics but four segments of one chain. Each stands on its own; read together they form a complete path:

**Strata → Criteria → Process → Attribution**

| # | Article | Position in the chain | The question it answers | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [How Translation Reshaped Chinese: Five Historical Waves and Modern Prose](articles/翻译如何重塑中文_两千年来五波外来语与现代写作真相.html) | **Foundation** · input side | What shaped Chinese | 🔴 `#E3120B` |

### Principles of the Inquiries

1. **Empirical Foundations First**: Every percentage, particle density, and quote is tied to verifiable corpus snapshots or primary publications.
2. **Explicit Epistemic Boundaries**: Hypotheses and observations are strictly demarcated; individual stylometric quirks are never dressed up as universal laws.
3. **Trilingual by Default**: Every essay ships in Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and English, side by side, switchable at the top.
4. **HTML-Native Visuals**: All figures are rendered as pure, vector-sharp HTML/SVG artifacts rather than lossy bitmaps. They are fully interactive, selectable, and readable on high-DPI displays.

### The Name

*White Stone Spring* takes its name from a quiet brook running over white stones. The stones are white, the water is transparent, nothing artificially tall grows there, and it puts on no pretensions. Writing is the practice of keeping to that clarity.
