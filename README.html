命名由来

「白石溪」取自山水里一条不起眼的名字：石头是白的，水是真的，不长高东西，也不装。写作用来记这条路。

English Introduction

"It's all made up, but you get to make it up."
A continuous inquiry into language, prose, and thought.

White Stone Spring (白石溪) is an open-access repository and publication venue dedicated to long-form essays on Chinese copywriting standards, historical linguistic shifts, and cognitive mechanisms in the era of artificial intelligence. Every essay is strictly grounded in reproducible quantitative data and primary sources, rejecting vacuous rhetoric in favor of verifiable corpus metrics.

Principles of the Inquiries

  1. Empirical Foundations First: Every percentage, particle density, and quote is tied to verifiable corpus snapshots or primary publications.
  2. Explicit Epistemic Boundaries: Hypotheses and observations are strictly demarcated; individual stylometric quirks are never dressed up as universal laws.
  3. HTML-Native Visuals: All figures, decks, and intellectual lineage maps are rendered as pure, vector-sharp HTML/SVG artifacts rather than lossy bitmaps. They are fully interactive, selectable, and readable on high-DPI displays.

The Name

White Stone Spring takes its name from a quiet brook running over white stones. The stones are white, the water is transparent, nothing artificially tall grows there, and it puts on no pretensions. Writing is the practice of keeping to that clarity.
