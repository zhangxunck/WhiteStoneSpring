写作系列：四篇一条链

当前连载的是一个系列，母题是用写作来学习和开发自己。四篇不是四个独立话题，是同一条链上的四段——每篇独立读得通，连起来是一条完整的路：

地层 → 判据 → 工序 → 归属

2 判据 · 输出端 写完怎么判断它是好的 🟢 #005A36
3 工序 · 转换动作 怎么把别人的话变成自己的判断 ⬛ #1A1A1A
4 归属 · 存储位置 · 终篇 判断力最终存在哪 🔵 #2C3E50

第 3 篇是这条链的枢纽——它把前两篇的成果接成三道可执行的工序（原子化 → 挂网 → 成文），并说明这个系列本身就是这套工序的产出。四篇之间的关联在那篇里交代。

The currently running series has a single theme — using writing to learn and develop yourself. The four pieces are not four independent topics but four segments of one chain. Each stands on its own; read together they form a complete path:

Strata → Criteria → Process → Attribution

# Article Position in the chain The question it answers Accent
1 Foundation · input side What shaped Chinese 🔴 #E3120B
2 Criterion · output side How to judge a finished piece of writing 🟢 #005A36
3 Process · the conversion act How to turn other people's words into your own judgment ⬛ #1A1A1A
4 Attribution · storage · final Where judgment ultimately lives 🔵 #2C3E50

Part 3 is the hinge of the chain. It takes the results of the first two and turns them into three executable processes (atomization → weaving → writing), and shows that the series itself is an output of those processes. How the four relate is explained there.

命名由來

「白石溪」取自山水裡一條不起眼的名字：石頭是白的，水是真的，不長高東西，也不裝。寫作用來記這條路。

命名由来

「白石溪」取自山水里一条不起眼的名字：石头是白的，水是真的，不长高东西，也不装。写作用来记这条路。

White Stone Spring takes its name from a quiet brook running over white stones. The stones are white, the water is transparent, nothing artificially tall grows there, and it puts on no pretensions. Writing is the practice of keeping to that clarity.
